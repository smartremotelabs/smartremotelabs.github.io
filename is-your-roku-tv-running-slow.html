Is Your Roku TV Running Slow? Try This Simple Restart Trick

Is your Roku TV suddenly feeling slow, laggy, or unresponsive?

Apps may take longer to open, menus might feel sluggish, or your Roku may take longer than usual to respond to the remote. Before you consider a factory reset or start deleting apps, there's a simple trick you can try first.

In this guide, we'll show you how to restart your Roku using a specific sequence of remote button presses. This can help refresh the Roku system and clear temporary data that may be contributing to sluggish performance.

And the best part?

It is not a factory reset. You won't have to reinstall your apps, reconnect your accounts, or set up your Roku from scratch.

Why Is My Roku Running Slow?

Like other streaming devices, Roku devices can sometimes become less responsive over time.

There can be several reasons for this, including:

  • Temporary system data accumulating
  • Apps becoming less responsive
  • Background processes
  • Network connection problems
  • Software that needs to be refreshed
  • A Roku device that has been running for a long time without a restart

A slow Roku doesn't necessarily mean that something is seriously wrong with the device.

Sometimes, a simple restart is all it needs.

How to Restart a Roku and Refresh the System

You can restart your Roku using the remote without performing a factory reset.

Step 1: Turn on your Roku

Make sure your Roku TV or Roku player is powered on and that you're on the Roku home screen.

Step 2: Use your Roku remote

Using the remote, enter the button sequence shown in our video.

The sequence is:

Home ×5 → Up ×1 → Rewind ×2 → Fast Forward ×2

Press the buttons in order and allow the Roku a few moments to respond.

Your Roku should restart automatically.

Tip: Press each button deliberately rather than holding the buttons down.

Step 3: Wait for the Roku to restart

The screen may go blank temporarily while the Roku restarts.

Give it a little time to complete the process. Once the Roku comes back up, try navigating through the menus and opening an app.

Is This the Same as a Factory Reset?

No.

This is an important distinction.

A factory reset completely wipes the Roku and returns it to its initial setup state. You would then need to reconnect the device, sign into your Roku account, reinstall channels/apps, and configure your settings again.

The restart method described here does not perform a factory reset.

Your installed apps and normal Roku setup should remain intact.

That's why it's worth trying this simple restart before considering a factory reset.

Will This Clear the Roku Cache?

Roku doesn't provide users with a traditional "Clear Cache" button like you might find on an Android phone.

However, restarting the Roku can clear temporary system data and refresh processes that may be contributing to sluggish behavior.

That's why you'll often see this button sequence described online as a Roku cache-clearing trick.

The important thing to remember is that it is essentially a system restart/refresh—not a factory reset.

What If My Roku Is Still Slow?

If your Roku is still running slowly after the restart, there are a few other things worth checking.

1. Check Your Internet Connection

A slow internet connection can make streaming apps appear slow even when the Roku itself is working normally.

Try another device on the same Wi-Fi network to see whether websites or streaming services are also experiencing problems.

2. Restart Your Network Equipment

If other devices are also experiencing connection problems, restarting your router and modem may help.

3. Check for Roku Software Updates

Make sure your Roku is running the latest available software. Software updates can include performance improvements and bug fixes.

4. Remove Apps You Don't Use

If you have many channels installed that you never use, removing unnecessary ones can help keep your Roku experience organized.

5. Consider the Age of Your Roku

Older Roku hardware may naturally feel slower as streaming apps become more demanding.

If your device is several years old and continues to struggle even after troubleshooting, the hardware itself may be a limiting factor.

Try the Restart Before a Factory Reset

If your Roku TV has become sluggish, don't jump straight to a factory reset.

A simple restart using the Roku remote is a much less disruptive first step. It takes only a few seconds to enter the button sequence and, importantly, you don't have to reinstall your apps afterward.

So the next time your Roku starts feeling slow, try the restart trick shown in our video.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does restarting Roku delete my apps?

No. The restart method described in this article is not a factory reset, so it does not require you to reinstall your apps.

Does this completely clear the Roku cache?

Roku does not provide a conventional cache-clearing option. Restarting the device can refresh the system and clear temporary data, which may help with sluggish performance.

Do I need to unplug my Roku?

Not for this method. The restart can be initiated using the Roku remote button sequence shown above.

Will this fix every slow Roku?

Not necessarily. A restart can help with temporary performance issues, but persistent problems may be caused by your internet connection, outdated software, or older Roku hardware.

Is a Roku factory reset necessary if my device is slow?

Usually, a factory reset shouldn't be your first step. Try restarting the Roku and checking your network and software first. A factory reset should generally be considered only after other troubleshooting steps haven't helped.

Final Thoughts

A slow Roku can be frustrating, especially when apps take longer to open or the interface doesn't respond as quickly as it should.

Fortunately, you don't always need to perform a factory reset.

Try the remote restart sequence first, give your Roku a moment to refresh, and see if performance improves.

It's quick, doesn't require reinstalling your apps, and is a much easier first troubleshooting step than resetting your entire device.
