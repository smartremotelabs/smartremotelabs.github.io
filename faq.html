Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What is Aruk: TV Remote Control?

Aruk: TV Remote Control is a free app for Android, iPhone, and iPad that controls a Roku TV or streaming player from your device. It works as a complete replacement for your physical TV remote, providing all the controls you need without batteries or lost remotes.

Is Aruk: TV Remote Control free?

Yes. Aruk: TV Remote Control is free to download and includes essential remote features such as navigation, keyboard input, and quick TV device connection. There are no ads on the remote control tab.

How do I download the app?

On Android, get Aruk: TV Remote Control from Google Play. On iPhone or iPad, get it from the App Store. Setup steps and screenshots are on the Android page and the iOS page.

Is the app available for iOS?

Yes. Aruk: TV Remote Control is available for iPhone and iPad. It finds Roku TVs and streaming players on your Wi-Fi and includes a full remote, voice search, a keyboard, custom shortcuts, and a Home Screen widget. See the iOS app page for setup steps and screenshots. The Android app is still on Google Play.

Compatibility & Setup

Which TV devices are supported?

Aruk: TV Remote Control works with:

  • Roku TV (all models)
  • Streaming Stick
  • Streaming Stick+
  • Ultra
  • Express
  • Premiere
  • Any device running Roku OS 5.0 or higher

What Android version do I need?

Aruk: TV Remote Control requires Android 5.0 (API level 21) or higher. Most modern Android devices meet this requirement. If your device is very old, you may need to update your Android OS to use the app.

Do my phone and TV need to be on the same WiFi network?

Yes. Your phone or tablet and the Roku have to be on the same Wi-Fi network, whether you are using Android, iPhone, or iPad. The app talks to the Roku over your local network, not through the internet. On iPhone and iPad, allow Local Network access when the system asks.

Can I use the app on multiple devices?

Yes. You can install Aruk on more than one Android phone, iPhone, or iPad and use each one to control the same Roku. That is useful when several people in the house have their own device.

Features & Functionality

Can I use this app without a physical remote?

Yes, absolutely! That's the main purpose of Aruk: TV Remote Control. You don't need a physical remote at all. The app provides all the functionality of the original TV remote.

Does the app support all remote functions?

Yes, Aruk: TV Remote Control supports all major remote functions including:

  • Navigation (Up, Down, Left, Right)
  • OK/Select button
  • Home button
  • Back button
  • Volume control
  • Mute
  • Channel navigation
  • Quick app launch buttons

Can I control volume with the app?

Yes, supported Roku TVs allow volume control through the app. You can adjust volume up/down, mute, and unmute directly from your phone.

Does the app have a keyboard or search function?

Yes, Aruk: TV Remote Control includes a keyboard feature that allows you to easily type and search on your Roku TV, which is much more convenient than using the remote to enter text.

Can I launch apps from the app?

Yes, you can quickly launch your favorite apps directly from Aruk: TV Remote Control without navigating through the menu.

Troubleshooting

The app won't connect to my TV device. What should I do?

Here are some troubleshooting steps:

  1. Make sure your phone and TV are on the same WiFi network
  2. Restart your TV device (unplug for 30 seconds, then plug back in)
  3. Restart your phone
  4. Uninstall and reinstall the Aruk: TV Remote Control app
  5. Check your router/firewall settings - make sure it's not blocking local connections

The app is very slow or laggy. How can I improve performance?

Try these optimization tips:

  • Make sure you have a strong WiFi signal on both devices
  • Close other apps running in the background on your phone
  • Reduce the distance between your phone and WiFi router
  • Restart your router and TV device
  • Update to the latest version of Aruk: TV Remote Control

Why does my phone keep disconnecting from my TV?

This is usually due to WiFi connectivity issues. Try:

  • Moving closer to your WiFi router
  • Restarting your router and TV device
  • Checking for WiFi interference from other devices
  • Updating your router firmware

If problems persist, contact us for support.

What should I do if a specific button doesn't work?

Some TV devices may have different capabilities. If a button doesn't work:

  1. Make sure your TV device supports that function
  2. Try restarting the app and your TV device
  3. Check that you're using the latest version of the app
  4. Contact us with the device model and button that's not working

Privacy & Security

Is my data secure?

Yes, your data is secure. Aruk: TV Remote Control only communicates with your TV device over your local WiFi network. We do not send any personally identifiable information to external servers. We only collect anonymized analytics and crash reports.

What data does the app collect?

Aruk: TV Remote Control collects minimal data:

  • Basic analytics to improve the app (no personal information)
  • Crash reports to identify and fix issues
  • We never collect passwords, personal details, or watch history

See our Privacy Policy for more details.

Is there a password required to use the app?

No, there is no password required. The app connects directly to your TV device on your local network. You don't need to create an account or log in.
